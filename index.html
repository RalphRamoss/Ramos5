FIVE BEAUTIFUL SPOTS IN THE PHILIPPINES

El Nido, Palawan

Famous for its clear waters, limestone cliffs, lagoons and beautiful islands.

Boracay

Known for its beautiful White Beach, clear water and relaxing island atmosphere.

Banaue Rice Terraces

Amazing rice terraces carved into the mountains of Ifugao.

Bohol

Home of the famous Chocolate Hills, beaches and unique wildlife.

Siargao

A beautiful island famous for surfing, beaches, lagoons and island hopping.
